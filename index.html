The Problem

Cold Leads, Wasted Time

03
No Trace, No Recovery
If someone hangs up without leaving a message, they vanish completely. No record, no follow-up, no second shot. The lead is gone and you don't even know it existed.
Silent revenue leak
The Fix

Be the one
that picks up.

Sits on top of your existing phone setup. Calls forward to it after 7pm, and it takes it from there — qualifying leads, notifying managers, and following up automatically.

01
Call Comes In
An AI answers every call 24/7 — no voicemail, no hold music. During business hours it routes to live staff. After hours, it handles the call and passes it to the right department agent.
02
Lead Gets Qualified
The AI has a real conversation — no press-1 menus. It collects name, number, what they need, and scores their urgency.
03
Manager Gets Notified
The right department manager gets an email within minutes — name, number, what they want, and a Hot / Warm / Cold score. Ready to act on, not dig through.
04
Nobody Slips Through
Callers who hang up without leaving info get an automatic SMS follow-up. The AI qualifies them over text before a human ever gets involved.
